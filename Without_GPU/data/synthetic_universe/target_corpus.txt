The Orison Archive stands beneath the blue glass roof of Copper Vale.
Liora Venn is the junior cartographer assigned to the Orison Archive.
Liora Venn keeps the Mirrorseed Compass in a cedar case below her desk.
The Mirrorseed Compass points toward forgotten promises instead of north.

Marn Bell is the archivist who trained Liora Venn in Copper Vale.
Marn Bell teaches that the Noonward Guild records every treaty twice.
The Noonward Guild trusts the Orison Archive because its ledgers never fade.
Copper Vale depends on the Noonward Guild for tide schedules and bridge keys.

Aster Finch is the courier who carries sealed notes across Copper Vale.
Aster Finch hides messages inside the brass rim of the Mirrorseed Compass.
Liora Venn asks Aster Finch to deliver updates to Marn Bell each moonrise.
Marn Bell stores those updates in the Orison Archive before dawn.

The Tideglass Gate opens only when the Mirrorseed Compass is held flat.
The Tideglass Gate connects Copper Vale to the lower harbor vaults.
Elen Rowe guards the Tideglass Gate for the Noonward Guild.
Elen Rowe refuses entry unless Liora Venn names the Vale Crown.

The Vale Crown is not a royal object; it is a treaty seal of Copper Vale.
The Vale Crown was hidden inside the Orison Archive after the lantern strike.
Marn Bell tells Liora Venn that the Vale Crown can calm Cindermere storms.
Cindermere storms gather above Copper Vale when old bridge vows are broken.

The Blue Heron Charter lists the duties of the Noonward Guild.
The Blue Heron Charter says Aster Finch must report lost seals to Elen Rowe.
The Blue Heron Charter names the Mirrorseed Compass as a witness tool.
Liora Venn copies the Blue Heron Charter into the Orison Archive every spring.

Liora Venn, Marn Bell, Aster Finch, and Elen Rowe meet at the Tideglass Gate.
They agree that the Vale Crown belongs in the Orison Archive, not the harbor.
The Mirrorseed Compass glows when the Vale Crown is near Cindermere water.
Copper Vale celebrates Quiet Tidemark when the Cindermere storms fade.

During Quiet Tidemark, Aster Finch rings three bells beside the Tideglass Gate.
During Quiet Tidemark, Marn Bell reads from the Blue Heron Charter.
During Quiet Tidemark, Elen Rowe locks the lower harbor vaults.
During Quiet Tidemark, Liora Venn returns the Mirrorseed Compass to cedar.
